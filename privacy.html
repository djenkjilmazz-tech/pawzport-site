🐾 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 (Android disclosures); June 5, 2026 (iOS)

Pawzport+ ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Pawzport+ mobile application.

Android edition — updated October 9, 2026

The following disclosures apply to the Android edition and take precedence over the iOS-specific description below.

  • Local records: Pet profiles, selected profile photos, microchip numbers, health notes and emergency contact details are stored in the app's private storage on your device. This release does not provide cloud backup. Archiving preserves records. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes local records. An emergency card is shared only when you choose a destination in Android's share sheet.
  • Optional AI: AI photo analysis and chat require your explicit in-app consent. When you submit a request, the selected photo or message and up to eight recent chat messages are sent over HTTPS through our Cloudflare service to Groq for processing. Health records, microchip numbers and emergency contacts are not automatically sent. Do not include information you do not want processed by these providers. Photo analysis describes visible traits; it does not identify an individual animal. AI is educational and is not veterinary diagnosis or treatment.
  • AI retention and reporting: Our service does not store ordinary chat messages or photos. Provider processing is subject to Cloudflare's and Groq's privacy policies. If you explicitly report a response, only that selected response and your chosen reason are stored by us for up to 30 days to investigate unsafe or incorrect content. Avoid reporting personal information you do not wish to share. You can withdraw AI consent in the app; this clears the current conversation and stops further AI requests.
  • Service protection: Our service receives your IP address when you connect. Daily salted hashes of the network and installation/customer identifiers, together with request counters, are used to enforce usage limits. Raw IP addresses, photos and chat messages are not saved in those counters.
  • Purchases: Google Play processes Android subscriptions. RevenueCat receives purchase/receipt information and an app-specific pseudonymous customer identifier to verify Premium access and restore purchases. Our AI service checks the corresponding subscription entitlement with RevenueCat. We do not receive payment-card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.
  • Permissions and features: Android uses the system photo picker and optional notification permission for local care reminders. This Android release has no advertising or separate analytics SDK, GPS tracking, noseprint identification, or veterinary booking service.

The Android edition is intended for users aged 13 and above. For privacy questions or requests about a reported response or purchase-related identifier, contact cnkylmz35@gmail.com. Local pet records are not accessible to us. Provider and store records are also subject to the relevant provider's retention and deletion processes.

iOS edition

1. Information We Collect

  • Pet Information: Names, breeds, photos, health records, and microchip numbers you enter for your pets.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we use location services for GPS tracking and nearby vet/clinic features. Location data is processed on-device and not stored on our servers.
  • Camera & Photos: Camera access is used for breed scanning, nose print identification, and pet photos. Images are processed on-device using our AI models.
  • Device Information: We collect anonymous device identifiers for analytics and crash reporting.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Pawzport+ app features
  • To process and manage your Premium subscription
  • To display relevant advertisements (free tier only, via Google AdMob)
  • To send push notifications about vaccination reminders (with your permission)
  • To improve our AI breed recognition and health features

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: Subscription management. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Advertising (free tier). Privacy Policy
  • The Dog API / The Cat API: Breed information lookup.
  • xAI Grok API: AI veterinary chat responses.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your pet health data is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage frameworks. We use industry-standard encryption for any data transmitted to our servers. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

Pawzport+ is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at cnkylmz35@gmail.com. You can also delete all app data by uninstalling the application.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.
